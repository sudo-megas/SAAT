# SAAT for Android v1.1

## Pick for me

Can't decide what to wear? The calendar now has a fourth way to log today,
alongside the home-screen widget, the launcher shortcut and the detail page
button: a **Pick for me** button under the month's stats.

Tap it and the app suggests a watch — **Random** picks evenly across
everything you own; **Weighted** leans toward whatever you haven't worn in a
while, without ever fully ruling out something you wore yesterday. Switch
between the two whenever you like; your choice is remembered.

Don't like the answer? **Re-roll** as many times as you want — nothing is
recorded until you tap **Wore this today**. Only owned watches are ever
suggested, the same rule the calendar already follows everywhere else.

This is the same feature the desktop app has had since v1.7.5, now on the
phone. The desktop's weekly planner is not part of this release — today only.

## Everything else

Unchanged from v1.0. See the [v1.0 release notes](android-1.0.md) for
